Privacy Policy

VidoByte Smart Video Compressor (Free & Pro) for Windows

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VidoByte Smart Video Compressor ("VidoByte", "the app"), offered by BenWynn ("we", "us"), handles information when you use its Windows video compression features, including the optional VidoByte Pro upgrade.

1. Video files and local processing

VidoByte accesses only the video files and folders that you choose or add to its queue. This access is used to read technical metadata (such as duration, file size, dimensions, and codec), estimate compression, and produce video outputs. Media processing uses local software tools, including FFmpeg/FFprobe where available.

Your source videos, audio, and generated video files are processed locally on your Windows device. The current version does not upload these files or their contents to servers operated by BenWynn. The original source file is not intentionally overwritten by the compression workflow.

2. Information stored on your device

VidoByte may save local preferences such as light/dark theme, the chosen batch output directory, batch compression mode, selected target percentage, and whether to skip optimized files. These settings are stored in a local settings file under %LOCALAPPDATA%\VidoByte\settings.json.

During encoding or analysis, the app may create temporary video segments, encoding-pass data, and incomplete output files on your device. It attempts to remove temporary processing files when tasks finish or are cancelled, but some may remain after an unexpected crash. Completed video exports remain in the destination folder you choose until you remove them.

3. Data collection, analytics, and advertising

Based on the app functionality currently implemented, VidoByte does not run a developer-operated analytics service, advertising network, account registration service, or video-upload service. We do not sell your video files or personal information. This statement does not cover independent data processing by Windows, Microsoft Store, or any tools and services you choose to use outside VidoByte.

4. Microsoft Store purchases and licensing

When running as a Microsoft Store-installed app, VidoByte may contact Microsoft Store services through Windows Store APIs to verify Pro entitlement on startup or when you check, purchase, or restore Pro. Payment, Microsoft account management, and Store transaction processing are handled by Microsoft, not by VidoByte. The app does not request or store your payment-card details.

Microsoft may process account, license, diagnostic, and transaction information under its own privacy practices. For details, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We may receive Store-level sales and financial reports through our developer account, but the app does not independently collect payment-card data.

5. Network access and third parties

Video compression does not require an internet connection to a BenWynn server. Microsoft Store purchases, license checks, downloads, and updates may require internet access. Third-party encoding components run locally as part of video processing; this policy does not replace their separate license terms.

6. Security and retention

Your files and local preferences remain on your computer and are subject to the security controls and account permissions of Windows. You control which media files are selected and where output files are saved. We do not maintain a developer-hosted collection of your media to retain or delete. Local files may remain until you delete them, and Windows backup or synchronization features may apply independently if you enable them.

7. Your choices and controls

  • You decide which videos and folders VidoByte may process.
  • You can delete video outputs and remaining temporary files using Windows File Explorer.
  • You can reset local application preferences by closing the app and deleting its local settings file mentioned in Section 2.
  • You can choose not to buy Pro; Free features remain available according to the app's current offering.

8. Children and changes to this policy

VidoByte is a general-purpose media utility and is not designed to collect children's personal information. If we add new features that change how data is processed, we will update this policy and its effective date before or when those changes become available.
